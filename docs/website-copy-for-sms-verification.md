# encmotion.com: page copy for SMS verification

Twilio and the phone carriers check the business website on the verification form. Right now encmotion.com shows a Squarespace "We're under construction" page, and reviewers usually reject parked or under-construction sites. Publish a simple one-page site with the three sections below before you submit. Squarespace: *Pages → Home → Edit*, then add the text. Add *SMS Terms* and *Privacy Policy* as their own pages and link them in the footer.

The name, address and email below match the Twilio form. Keep them identical. Update the date when you publish.

The domain (encmotion.com) doesn't contain the business name, so the legal name **Creative Connection by Christy, Inc.** appears at the top of the page. Reviewers compare the website against the name on the form.

## SMS Terms

**Market Pager team alerts**

Creative Connection by Christy, Inc. sends text message alerts to members of its market showroom team during furniture market events. These alerts tell a rep when a dealer is ready to see their product division. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

- **Who receives messages:** only showroom team members who opt in themselves. On first sign-in to our Market Pager app, each member enters their cell number and checks a box agreeing to these terms. We record the time and wording of each agreement.
- **Frequency:** varies. Messages are sent only during market weeks, typically a few to a few dozen per day.
- **Costs:** message and data rates may apply.
- **Opt out:** reply **STOP** at any time. You'll get one confirmation and no further messages.
- **Help:** reply **HELP**, or email jrmoore@encmotion.com.
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy Policy

Creative Connection by Christy, Inc. collects the name, email address and mobile phone number of our market showroom team members so we can send market alerts. We use this information only to operate those alerts.

**We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.** The information is shared only with service providers that deliver the messages for us (for example, our SMS provider), and only for that purpose.

To ask about, update or delete your information, email jrmoore@encmotion.com.
